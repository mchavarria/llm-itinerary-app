# LLM Itinerary App

The **LLM Itinerary App** aims to make travel planning more organized and centralized by gathering all your travel-related bookings and information in one place. Here’s what the app is designed to do:

## Overview

- **Centralized Booking Management**: Automatically collects travel bookings from email confirmations, such as flights, train tickets, hotel reservations, and more.
- **Calendar-like Interface**: View all your travels and bookings in an intuitive calendar-like overview.
- **Simple and Local**: 
  - Designed as a simple web app.
  - Utilizes browser storage for data management in the initial phase, allowing you to keep all your data locally and privately.

## Vision

The goal is to create a hassle-free travel assistant that intelligently organizes your travel information, making your planning effortless and saving you time.
